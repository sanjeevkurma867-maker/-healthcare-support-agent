# Prior Authorization: What Members Need to Know
This guide explains prior authorization for members. All content is fictional and used for a prototype.

## What prior authorization means
Prior authorization is approval from the plan before you receive certain services. Without it the plan may deny the claim.

## Which services need it
Services that usually need prior authorization include advanced imaging such as MRI and CT scans, surgery, extended physical therapy and specialty drugs. Primary care visits and preventive care do not need it.

## Who requests it
Your provider submits the request. You do not need to file it yourself.

## How long it takes
A standard request is decided within 7 business days. An urgent request is decided within 72 hours.

## If a request is denied
You or your provider can appeal within 180 calendar days. See the Appeals and Grievances Guide.
