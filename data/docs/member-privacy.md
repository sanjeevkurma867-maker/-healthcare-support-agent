# Member Privacy and Rights
This guide explains how your information is handled. All content is fictional and used for a prototype.

## Your records
You can see your own claims and benefits. You cannot see another member's records, and no one can see yours without a reason connected to your care or your request.

## What the assistant can and cannot do
The assistant gives administrative information about benefits, claims and paperwork. It does not give medical advice, and it does not approve or deny claims or authorizations.

## Getting help from a person
You can ask for a support representative at any time. The assistant can pass your question to a person with a short summary so you do not have to repeat yourself.
