# SynCare Gold Plan Benefits Guide 2026
This guide describes the Gold plan for plan year 2026. All figures are fictional and used for a prototype.

## Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum
The Gold plan deductible is $1,500 for an individual. The deductible is the amount you pay for covered services before the plan starts to pay. The out-of-pocket maximum is $5,000 for an individual. After you reach the out-of-pocket maximum, the plan pays 100% of covered services for the rest of the plan year.

## Doctor visit costs
A primary care visit costs a $20 copay. A specialist visit costs a $40 copay. Preventive care such as an annual check-up is covered at 100% with no copay when you use an in-network provider.

## Urgent care and emergency room
An urgent care visit costs a $75 copay. An emergency room visit costs a $250 copay. The emergency room copay is waived if you are admitted to the hospital.

## Coinsurance
After you meet the deductible, the Gold plan pays 80% of covered services and you pay 20% coinsurance until you reach the out-of-pocket maximum.

## Prescription drugs
Generic drugs cost a $10 copay for a 30-day supply. Preferred brand drugs cost a $45 copay for a 30-day supply.

## Virtual visits
Virtual visits with in-network providers cost $0 on the Gold plan.

## Imaging and diagnostic tests
Advanced imaging such as MRI and CT scans is covered after the deductible is met, and you pay 20% coinsurance. Advanced imaging requires prior authorization.
