# Virtual Care Guide
This guide explains virtual visits. All content is fictional and used for a prototype.

## Availability
Virtual visits are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week through the member app and website.

## What virtual visits cover
Virtual visits are offered for everyday needs and for behavioral health counselling. Virtual visits are not for emergencies. In an emergency, go to the nearest emergency room.

## Cost and authorization
The cost of a virtual visit depends on your plan and is listed in your plan benefits guide. Virtual visits do not need prior authorization.
