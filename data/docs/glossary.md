# Plain-Language Insurance Glossary
This glossary explains common insurance words. All content is fictional and used for a prototype.

## Deductible
The deductible is the amount you pay for covered health services each year before the plan starts to pay.

## Copay
A copay is a fixed amount you pay for a covered service, such as a doctor visit or a prescription.

## Coinsurance
Coinsurance is the percentage of the cost of a covered service that you pay after you meet your deductible.

## Out-of-pocket maximum
The out-of-pocket maximum is the most you pay for covered services in a plan year. After you reach it, the plan pays 100% of covered services.

## Premium
A premium is the amount you pay each month to keep your plan active.

## In-network
In-network providers have a contract with the plan, so your costs are lower when you use them.

## Out-of-network
Out-of-network providers do not have a contract with the plan, so you may pay more or the service may not be covered.

## Explanation of Benefits
An Explanation of Benefits, or EOB, is a statement that shows how the plan processed a claim. An EOB is not a bill.

## Prior authorization
Prior authorization is approval from the plan that you may need before you receive certain services or drugs.

## Appeal
An appeal is a request that the plan look again at a decision it made, such as a denied claim.

## Formulary
A formulary is the list of prescription drugs that the plan covers.

## Referral
A referral is a written recommendation from your primary care provider to see a specialist or to get a test.
