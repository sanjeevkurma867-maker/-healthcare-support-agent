# SynCare Gold Plan Benefits Guide 2025 (replaced)
This guide described the Gold plan for plan year 2025 and has been replaced by the 2026 guide.

## Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum
The Gold plan deductible was $1,200 for an individual. The out-of-pocket maximum was $4,500 for an individual.

## Doctor visit costs
A primary care visit cost a $15 copay. A specialist visit cost a $30 copay.
