# Appeals and Grievances Guide
This guide explains how to appeal a claim decision. All content is fictional and used for a prototype.

## Appeal deadline
An appeal must be filed within 180 calendar days of the date on the Explanation of Benefits. Appeals filed after 180 days may not be reviewed.

## How long an appeal takes
A standard appeal is decided within 30 calendar days of receipt. An expedited appeal is decided within 72 hours when a delay could seriously harm your health.

## Who can appeal
A member can appeal a claim decision. A provider can appeal for a member with the member's written consent.

## What to include in an appeal
Include the claim number, a copy of the EOB, a short explanation of why you disagree, and any supporting documents such as a referral or clinical notes from your provider.

## Complaints about service
A grievance is a complaint about service rather than a decision about a claim. Grievances are acknowledged within 5 business days.
