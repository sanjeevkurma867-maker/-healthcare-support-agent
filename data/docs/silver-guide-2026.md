# SynCare Silver Plan Benefits Guide 2026
This guide describes the Silver plan for plan year 2026. All figures are fictional and used for a prototype.

## Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum
The Silver plan deductible is $3,000 for an individual. The deductible is the amount you pay for covered services before the plan starts to pay. The out-of-pocket maximum is $7,500 for an individual. After you reach the out-of-pocket maximum, the plan pays 100% of covered services for the rest of the plan year.

## Doctor visit costs
A primary care visit costs a $35 copay. A specialist visit costs a $65 copay. Preventive care such as an annual check-up is covered at 100% with no copay when you use an in-network provider.

## Urgent care and emergency room
An urgent care visit costs a $100 copay. An emergency room visit costs a $350 copay. The emergency room copay is waived if you are admitted to the hospital.

## Coinsurance
After you meet the deductible, the Silver plan pays 70% of covered services and you pay 30% coinsurance until you reach the out-of-pocket maximum.

## Prescription drugs
Generic drugs cost a $15 copay for a 30-day supply. Preferred brand drugs cost a $60 copay for a 30-day supply.

## Virtual visits
Virtual visits with in-network providers cost a $10 copay on the Silver plan.

## Imaging and diagnostic tests
Advanced imaging such as MRI and CT scans is covered after the deductible is met, and you pay 30% coinsurance. Advanced imaging requires prior authorization.
