# Pharmacy Benefits Guide
This guide explains prescription drug benefits. All content is fictional and used for a prototype.

## Drug tiers
Drugs are grouped into tiers. Tier 1 is generic drugs, Tier 2 is preferred brand drugs, Tier 3 is non-preferred brand drugs and Tier 4 is specialty drugs. The lower the tier, the lower your copay.

## Formulary
The formulary is the list of drugs the plan covers. A drug that is not on the formulary is not covered unless an exception is approved.

## Mail-order pharmacy
Mail-order pharmacy delivers a 90-day supply of a maintenance drug for the price of two 30-day copays.

## Refills
You can refill a prescription when about 80% of the previous supply has been used. Refills requested too early may be rejected.

## Specialty drugs
Specialty drugs must be filled through the plan's specialty pharmacy and need prior authorization.
