# Prior Authorization Requirements: Lumbar Spine MRI
This document lists what a lumbar spine MRI request must contain. All content is fictional and used for a prototype.

## Requirements
The request must include the member number, the requested service date and the ordering provider identifier. It must document at least 6 weeks of conservative therapy. It must include clinical notes that describe the back pain and any neurological findings. It must include a referral letter.

## Emergency imaging
Emergency imaging does not need prior authorization.
